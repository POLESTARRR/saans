What this cannot know

Readings are live and real. Hourly PM2.5 comes from Open-Meteo's air quality service, which serves the CAMS global model interpolated to your coordinates. It refreshes every time you open this page, so it is current whether you came back tomorrow or next winter.

A modelled field is not a monitor on your street. It is good at the shape of a city's day and approximate about your particular lane. Next to a construction site or an arterial road your real exposure is higher than any citywide figure.

Emission figures come from published studies, not your kitchen. Real homes vary enormously. Each source and its origin is listed in data/sources.json.

PM2.5 only. NO2 from gas burners, ozone and VOCs are real and are not modelled here.
